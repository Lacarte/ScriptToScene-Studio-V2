# Content Monetization Strategy

Source: Proven tips from active TikTok/FB/YT creators.

## Niche Selection
- Invest in niches where accounts with **few followers** have **viral videos (1M+ views)**
- This signals the algorithm rewards the content, not the creator

## TikTok Account Setup
- Only some countries allow monetization
- Option: buy a French account (~6 USD)
- Use dedicated device per account (never mix countries)
- **Never use VPN**

## Account Warming (Critical)
- 3-5 days of warming before posting
- Browse, like, comment — act like a real user
- Do NOT publish content during warming

## Content Strategy
- **Do not invent from scratch** at the start
- Replicate what already works in your niche
- Find videos with **500K+ views** published **less than 7 days ago**
- Reverse-engineer: hook, pacing, style, duration

## Publishing Cadence
- **1 video per day** per account
- Consistent schedule, same time each day

## Video Duration
- Before monetization: **under 1 minute**
- Sweet spot: **30-45 seconds**
- After monetization: can experiment with longer

## Engagement Optimization
- Remove all audio pauses/dead air
- Scenes: **max 3 seconds** each
- Increase animation speed
- Use camera movements (pan, zoom, tilt)
- Voice: dynamic, quality, not robotic
- Slightly increase voice speed (in TTS tool, not in editor)

## Quality
- Always export **1080p minimum**
- HD is non-negotiable for algorithm reach

## Multi-Platform
- Same video → TikTok + Facebook + YouTube Shorts
- 3x reach from 1x effort

## Upload Process (TikTok)
- Always upload from mobile
- Transfer: PC → Google Drive → Phone → Upload
- Avoids quality loss from other transfer methods

## Revenue Math
- 5 niches x 1 video/day = 5 videos/day
- 5 videos x 3 platforms = 15 posts/day
- At scale: batch produce weekly, schedule daily
